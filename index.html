Stitchlings

Stitchlings is a combat adventure playable alone or with a friend, set in a haunted stop motion set called The Workshop. You play as the titular Stitchlings, who have the ability to stitch toy-like parts dropped by enemies onto themselves to grant new abilities.

Wok This Way

Wok This Way is a 3D food themed 4v4 TPS online party game, where players use suction guns to collect ingredients that grant different abilities.

Teams compete to gather ingredients and decide whether to boost teammates, sabotage opponents, or deposit the ingredients into their team’s cooking pot.
